✅ Settled free
Turn messy Slack threads into clean decision records.

Paste a thread. Get back what was decided, who owns it, why, and what happens next. Runs locally with Ollama — your data never leaves your machine.

$ python3 settled.py --file thread.txt ## Decision 1 Use Stripe as the billing provider - Owner: Maya - Confidence: 🟢 high - Action items: - Tom: set up live account by Thursday - Priya: research VAT compliance by Friday

📌 Stamped free
Record decisions once. Never argue about them again.

Log a decision in 10 seconds. Pull it up in 2 when someone forgets. Stores everything locally in plain JSON — nothing leaves your machine.

$ python3 stamped.py add "Stripe approved. Owner: Tom." Stamped [a1b2c3d4] 2024-03-27 09:14 $ python3 stamped.py find stripe You decided: Stripe approved. Owner: Tom.
